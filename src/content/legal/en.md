## Who processes your data

## What data is collected

Only what you write in the contact form: name, email address, organisation (if you provide one) and the content of your message.

This website **uses no cookies, does not track your browsing and stores no data in any database.**

## What it is used for

Solely to read your message and reply to you. It is not used to send you advertising, it is not shared with third parties and it is not used for profiling.

## How it reaches me

The form is sent through the Web3Forms service, which forwards the content to the email address above and does not store it permanently. The message then sits in the mailbox like any other email.

## How long it is kept

For as long as needed to handle your enquiry and keep the conversation thread. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

## Your rights

You may request access to your data, or its correction or deletion, or object to its processing, by writing to **josebaerrazkin562@gmail.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
